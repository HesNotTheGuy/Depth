# Commercial Licensing

Depth is dual-licensed. The default license is **AGPL-3.0** (see [LICENSE](./LICENSE)). The AGPL is suitable for:

- Personal projects
- Open-source software (provided your project is also AGPL-compatible)
- Internal tooling at companies, **as long as the resulting product is not distributed or offered as a service**
- Academic and research use

If your use case involves any of the following, you need a commercial license:

- Embedding the Depth SDK in a proprietary application or product
- Offering Depth (or a derivative) as a hosted service or API
- Bundling Depth with closed-source software that is sold or distributed
- Removing AGPL obligations such as source disclosure for users of a hosted instance
- Any use where AGPL-3.0 terms are incompatible with your distribution model

A commercial license removes the copyleft requirements of the AGPL. You retain full freedom to integrate, distribute, and modify Depth without releasing source code under AGPL terms.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I evaluate Depth before buying a commercial license?**
Yes. Build prototypes and run internal evaluations under the AGPL terms. A commercial license is only required when you ship.

**I contribute to Depth. What does that mean for me?**
Contributions to the public repository are accepted under a Contributor License Agreement (CLA) that grants the project owner the right to relicense your contributions commercially. This is what allows the dual-licensing model to work and is standard practice for projects following the same model (MongoDB, Qt, Sentry).

**Can I fork Depth?**
Yes, under AGPL-3.0. You may not relicense the fork commercially, since you do not hold the copyright. If you want to monetize a derivative, contact us about a partnership or commercial license.

**Does the C++ SDK and the web app share the same license?**
Yes. Both are AGPL-3.0 by default and both are available under a commercial license.
